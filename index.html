How this works

Your phone or computer keeps a passkey for this site, locked by your fingerprint, face or PIN. When you unlock it, it hands this page a secret, and this page turns that secret into your nostr key. The same passkey always gives the same key.

Your key stays in this page only while it is open. It is never saved here and never sent anywhere. There is no server.
