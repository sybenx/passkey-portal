Handle test

This page checks one thing: can your passkey manager hold a small secret in the passkey itself and give it back unchanged? It makes no account and no nostr key. The value is random and only for this test.

Step 1. Make a test passkey. It is saved as "Handle test" with today's date.

    Step 2. Use it. This works after a reload too, or on another device your manager syncs to.

      The two "fingerprint" lines should match. A fingerprint is a short code worked out from the value, so the value itself is never shown. When you are done, delete the "Handle test" passkey in your passkey manager.
